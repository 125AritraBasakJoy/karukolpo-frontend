The Living Soul of Bengali Folk Traditions

Karukolpo was founded out of deep reverence for Bengal's centuries-old terracotta and clay artisan heritage. In traditional kumar paras (potters' colonies) and folk art studios, master sculptors and painters give form to devotion using river clay, bamboo armatures, and vibrant mineral pigments.

From the sacred protimas of Durga and Lakshmi to the ceremonial shoras hung in rural homes, our mission is to sustain these precious art forms and connect the master creators directly with lovers of authentic culture.

Ancestral Clay Sculpture

Honoring generations of Kumar sculptors who fashion sacred protimas with extraordinary patience and skill.

Hand-Painted Folk Shora

Keeping traditional Lakshmi shora folk painting vibrant, featuring sacred deities and rural motifs.

Terracotta & Earthenware

Celebrating baked and unglazed earthenware prodips and vessels shaped from natural riverbed clay.
