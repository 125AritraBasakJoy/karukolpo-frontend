Devotion & Heritage Shaped in River Clay

In Bengal’s legendary Kumar paras (potters' colonies), sacred clay sculpture and earthen craft are forms of worship. Master sculptors shape alluvial river soil into magnificent protimas of Durga and Lakshmi, alongside ceremonial earthen shoras and terracotta prodips.

Every creation is molded from natural earth, sun-dried, and hand-finished with traditional techniques, connecting generational artisans directly with lovers of authentic culture.
